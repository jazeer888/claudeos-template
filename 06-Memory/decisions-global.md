## Example (fictional)

## 2026-03-10 — Deprioritizing the enterprise-fleet segment
Context: Two enterprise fleets (200+ trucks) approached us wanting Northwind
Dispatch, but their procurement cycle and feature requests (SSO, custom
reporting, SLAs) would consume a quarter of engineering time for two logos.
Decision: Stay focused on the 5-30 truck segment through at least the next
funding round; route enterprise inbound to a waitlist, not a sales process.
Consequences: Slower logo-count growth in the short term; keeps the product
and team focused on the segment the whole thesis is built around.
