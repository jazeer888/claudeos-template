# Working Preferences

How you want Claude to communicate and work with you — tone, depth, format.
This is what makes output feel tailored instead of generic. Replace with your
own; the example below is one starting point, not a requirement.

**Example:**

- Concise, structured, execution-focused. No fluff, no generic advice.
- Prioritize real-world applicability, scalability, business impact over theory.
- Include trade-offs, risks, assumptions, and concrete next steps where relevant.
- Default to high-signal communication for decision-making and execution.
