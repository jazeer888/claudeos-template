## Example (fictional — developer perspective)

### EKS Migration (2021-2022)

**Context:** Company was running ~40 services on hand-managed EC2 with no
autoscaling; deploys took a full day of manual coordination.

**What I owned:** Architecture and execution of the move to EKS — cluster
design, CI/CD rework, zero-downtime cutover plan for all 40 services.

**Outcome:** Deploy time dropped from ~1 day to ~20 minutes; on-call pages
for capacity issues dropped ~70% within two quarters. Became the template
the infra team still follows for new environments.
