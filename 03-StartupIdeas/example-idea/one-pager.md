## Example (fictional — founder perspective)

# Northwind Dispatch — One Pager

**Problem:** Small freight brokers coordinate loads over phone calls and
spreadsheets — no visibility into truck availability until someone answers
the phone.

**Who has it:** Freight brokers with 5-30 trucks — too small for enterprise
TMS software, too big to keep running on spreadsheets.

**Why now:** Driver shortage has made utilization the #1 lever on margin;
brokers are actively looking for anything that cuts idle time.

**Why me:** 8 years building backend systems for a logistics marketplace —
know the data model and the objections a broker will raise before they raise
them.

**Next validation step:** 10 discovery calls with brokers in this size band,
validating that "real-time truck availability" is the #1 wish over
"automated invoicing" or "load matching."
