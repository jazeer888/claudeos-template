# Northwind Dispatch — Positioning

## One-liner

Real-time truck visibility for freight brokers too small for enterprise TMS
and too busy for spreadsheets.

## Who it's for

Freight brokers with 5-30 trucks — the segment enterprise TMS vendors don't
sell to and spreadsheets can't keep up with.

## Why now

Driver shortage has made utilization the #1 margin lever industry-wide;
brokers are actively shopping for anything that cuts idle time, not just
tolerating the status quo.

## Differentiation

Priced and onboarded for a broker with 10 trucks, not 500 — competitors
either ignore this segment or bury it in enterprise sales cycles.
