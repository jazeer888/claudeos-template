# Northwind Dispatch — Overview

**Problem:** Brokers running 5-30 trucks coordinate loads by phone and
spreadsheet — no visibility into which truck is free until someone picks up
the phone. This costs them idle miles and lost loads to competitors who
happen to call the right driver first.

**Solution:** A dispatcher dashboard showing live truck location and
availability, with one-tap load assignment that pushes straight to the
driver's phone.

**Target user:** Dispatchers and owner-operators at small brokerages —
not the large fleets already served by enterprise TMS platforms.

**Current stage:** 5 design partners live on manual GPS polling; building
real-time ingestion before opening up self-serve signup.
